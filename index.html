quick answers

Questions students & parents ask

What is Feynlearn?

Feynlearn is a learning-by-teaching platform for Class 9 and 10 students. You explain what you are learning to a peer in regular 20-minute sessions, using the Feynman Technique, so concepts actually stick.

Who is Feynlearn for?

Right now the pilot is for students in Classes 9 and 10. We plan to expand to Classes 6 to 12 and, later, to a free app for all kinds of learners.

What is the Feynman Technique?

It is a learning method named after Nobel Prize-winning physicist Richard Feynman: you only truly understand something when you can explain it simply to someone else. Where you get stuck explaining is exactly what you still need to learn.

How long is a Feynlearn session?

Each teach-to-peer session is 20 minutes. Feynlearn splits every chapter into small sub-topics and schedules a 20-minute session for each one automatically.

What happens in a teach-to-peer session?

You teach the topic to a student moderator who asks the right doubts and questions to pinpoint exactly where your understanding breaks down. A short daily quiz with detailed solutions follows to build exam readiness.

Is Feynlearn free?

Community sessions, where you attend sessions from anyone on the platform, are always free, and every learner receives a graphic tablet for free at onboarding. You can pre-register for pilot discounts using the form on this page.

How can I join the Feynlearn pilot?

Pre-register using the form on this page. The pilot is limited to 6 to 10 students from Classes 9 and 10 and is based in Pune, India. Parents can also email the founder with questions.
